Create a professional bespoke LOGOTYPE concept for a software developer tool. Exact uppercase spelling: DOUGONG, seven letters D O U G O N G. Make the two O letters identical and the two G letters identical. The client explicitly wants a fresh design unconstrained by the previous typography. This must look like an identity designer has drawn original letters, not simply typed a system font. Full horizontal lockup: the existing symbol from the reference on the LEFT, the custom DOUGONG wordmark as HERO, a quiet black rectangular badge with white NET on the RIGHT. The symbol in the reference is a fixed geometric three-component black shape, including a lower-left detached square; do not change it into a whale or knot. Clear separation between icon, wordmark and badge. One single lockup on a pure uniform white wide canvas, occupying about 80 percent width, vertically centered. Black only, no shadows, gradients, texture, mockups, labels, secondary versions or other words. Vector-like sharp contours, carefully resolved optical spacing, strong readability. DIRECTION A — STRUCTURAL, UPRIGHT, EXTENDED. Design a confident semi-extended uppercase wordmark with a broad, low, stable silhouette, approximately 7:1 word width to cap height. Medium-heavy strokes, subtle tension between flat structural sides and beautifully softened large curves; generous shaped counters in O, powerful D, an ownable horizontal joint in G, precise U and N. Letter widths are optically proportional, not monospaced. The design idea is architectural load-bearing and deliberate assembly. Keep a restrained premium technical identity, memorable from one or two specific letterform decisions. NOT the ordinary rounded-rectangle condensed font; NOT thin circular Futura; NOT an italic or decorative stencil. Original typography with authority, enough inner whitespace for small-size use.
